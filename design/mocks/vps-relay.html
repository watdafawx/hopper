Relay through a VPS

Run this on the VPS (as a user with sudo). It installs the relay and connects back here.

curl -fsSL …/hopperd-0.72.6-linux-arm64.tar.gz | sudo tar xz … && sudo hopperd relay join https://… --key hrk_9f2c…
Waiting for the VPS… (key good for 30 minutes)
Then open these ports in your cloud's firewall page: 25565/tcp, 25566/tcp, 24454/udp.

4 · Settings › Players & access: Online mode behind a proxy

Today: "Check players against Microsoft accounts. Turn off only for LAN testing; anyone could join as anyone." That's wrong and alarming behind the relay, where the relay checks accounts.

A · Locked switch, new line, link to the relayPick
The setting stays where people look for it; the line says who checks accounts and why it can't be turned on here (it would lock everyone out). Same treatment for the other three keys the relay sets.
Online mode
Accounts are checked by the relay (play.example.com). Players can't join this server directly. Relay settings online-mode
Secure chat profiles
Set by the relay. enforce-secure-profile
Whitelist
Only listed players can join. white-list
B · Grouped "Set by the relay" box

All four keys in one place with one explanation, but they move out of their usual sections, so search and the desktop's layout differ.

Set by the relay

The relay checks accounts, so these stay as the relay needs them. Stop using the relay to change them.

C · Editable, with a warning

Keeps full control, but flipping it on breaks every join with an odd error; a lock is kinder and "Play at home" covers the real need.

Online mode relay
Off because the relay checks accounts. Turning it on stops everyone joining through the relay. online-mode

Picks

  • Setup: A one dialog with a step list: also the repair screen, same height, checks-then-acts per step.
  • Status: A card on Overview: copyable address first, three stat numbers in the Overview style, red state with "Play at home".
  • Ping: A one total number (what players feel), split on hover.
  • Online mode: A locked switch in place with "Accounts are checked by the relay".

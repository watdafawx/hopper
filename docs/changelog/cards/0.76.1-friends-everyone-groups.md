# Friends: group members who aren't your friends show under "In your groups"

**Branch:** t3/friends-everyone-group-members   **Commit:** 6840f72   **Size:** S
**Apps:** desktop only (Hopper Server has no Friends panel)

## From -> to
- Before: the Everyone list only showed friends and people who shared a pack outside a group, so a group member you aren't friends with who shared nothing (Robin) was missing.
- After: everyone who said yes in a group you joined is listed under "In your groups" (once, never you, not people still invited); their row reads "In a group with you" and opens their page.

## Root cause
`FriendsPanel::people()` (src/gpui/friends.rs) built the list from `mailbox_friends()` plus the senders of non-group packs. It never read group members, so the "In your groups" section only ever held pack senders.

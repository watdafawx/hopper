## From -> to
- Before: you change a mod and it isn't shared yet (offline, a send that keeps failing, or "When I press Push"). A friend updates the same mod. After 10 seconds Hopper stopped waiting and threw the friend's update away without a word. If you then took your change back, you stayed on the old file for good. If you shared yours later, it went out as the newest and undid the friend's update for the whole group.
- After: the friend's update is kept. It goes in as soon as your own change is shared (right after it, on every PC) or as soon as you take your change back. The strip says so the whole time it waits.

What a player sees, in order, when the share comes late:
1. Strip: "Your change goes first" / "Waiting for your change to Sodium to be shared, then Mia's version goes in."
2. Your change goes out. For a moment the group has your file.
3. Mia's version goes in on your PC, then on everyone's. Mod rows say "Updated by Mia".

## How it works (short)
- When the 10 s wait runs out, the friend's change of that mod is saved on the copy (one per mod, newest wins) instead of dropped. Their code is still taken in, as before.
- It is made by the normal merge + `apply`, only once your own change of that mod is no longer unsent:
  - shared: dated right after your publish, so it wins everywhere; your PC then sends it on as the friend's change (no second Push);
  - taken back: made as it came.
- A newer change of the mod ends or replaces the kept one, so a mod the group removed later never comes back.
- No relay change, no new traffic, no timer: Hopper looks again only when the pack or its last publish changed. Nothing downloads before that merge.

## Notes for the maintainer
- **Deliberate behaviour to know about:** after a late share, the group is on your file for a moment (others download it), then on the friend's again (a second download for them). That is "ours is shared first, then theirs", as the brief asked. The other order (drop your unsent change at once) would lose the player's work without it ever reaching anyone.
- **Inside 10 s nothing changed:** if your change goes out before the wait runs out, yours wins as before (latest wins) and the friend's is dropped by the existing rule. Only the give-up path is new.
- **Mods only.** A loader, pack version or lock held too long is still dropped (now a `log::warn`). Moving a whole pack back by itself right after this PC updated it seemed too much for this fix. Say if those should be kept too.
- The kept change's date after your publish is "your publish's number + 1" (the relay isn't asked). It only has to beat your own publish on the other PCs; a real later change by anyone still wins over it.
- With members on a Hopper too old to send stamps, an echo of your publish looks like their own later change and ends the kept one: then yours stays (as before the fix). Not silent-worse than today.
- Found on the side, not fixed: `ui::tests::built_page_has_no_inline_script_or_style` fails in any worktree without a built web UI (the placeholder page has a `style=` attribute).
- `on_group_merged` replaces `held` from the merge's snapshot, like `owed` (the audit's minor note on `owed` applies here too).

**Branch:** t3/vps-relay-setup-trust   **Commits:** 3f2468ae, 952e3358 (+ merge of main 727ff0c1)   **Size:** M-L
**Apps:** Hopper Server only. Desktop has no relay status for a paired server's servers (B8): not done.

Mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/3bNUr98rESDuwaEgVNDKcB (picked Setup A, Health A).

## From -> to
- Before: setup was three steps (Connect, Check, Install) with nothing on what to rent; any Linux went ahead until something broke; the card showed up/down and "Try now", errors were system text.
- After: five steps (Before you start, Connect, Check, Install, Test). Debian 12/13 and Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 only, said up front; any other system stops at Check with what to do and "Nothing was changed on the VPS". The card shows Relay up/down and for how long, Ping from here, Players through it, Last test and "Test again"; errors are plain words. "Run again" keeps the relay's own ports.

# Skin tab: look up a player's skin by name, and every pose as a button

**Branch:** t3/59156ead   **Commit:** e71548c   **Size:** M
**Apps:** desktop only (Hopper Server has no accounts or skins)

## From -> to
- Before: copying a player's skin meant finding "From a link or name", which opened a hidden field; errors came as a toast; the preview said "nOtCh's skin" as typed. Poses were one "Walk" button with a drop-down menu.
- After: Change skin starts with a "Player name or link" field and a Look up button (Enter works too). The result shows under the field ("Notch's skin is on the preview" / "There's no Minecraft player named …"), the label uses Mojang's spelling, and the arms follow the player's model (Classic/Slim). Nothing changes on the account until Apply. All 17 poses show as segmented buttons with icons in their three groups, the chosen one in the selected tint.

Lookup bug check: typing a plain name already worked end to end (probe of the real `fetch_skin`: `Notch`, `jeb_`, `nOtCh`, `  Notch  `, `https://namemc.com/profile/Notch.1` all loaded, unknown name gave "There's no Minecraft player named …"). No broken lookup; the problems were findability, the typed-case label, toast-only errors, and NameMC links pasted without `https://` being refused (now read).

Mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/Evib3ArXcRJHN8mMBwRy1m (A: flat pose row + separate name field; B, picked: poses in their 3 groups + one name-or-link field replacing the toggle).

## Notes for the maintainer
- Hopper Dark's `danger` (#dc2626) is 3.87:1 on the dialog background; affects every `theme.danger` text (54 places), not just this. Worth a lighter dark-mode danger in `theme.rs` as its own change.
- The preview bar under a narrow preview truncates "Pix… / Th…" next to "Pick a skin to preview it here" (pre-existing, visible in after-idle-narrow-light.png).
- Movement group (7 poses) wraps to two rows at 1440 px wide; groups stay together rather than one flat row by design (mock B).

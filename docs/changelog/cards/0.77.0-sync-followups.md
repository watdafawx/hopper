# Friends: ask before getting a group pack, Try again after Hopper gives up

**Branch:** t3/friends-sync-followups   **Commit:** cd80866   **Size:** M
**Apps:** desktop only (Hopper Server has no friends or groups code)

## From -> to
- Before: with "Keep my packs the same as the group" on, Hopper downloaded every pack shared to the group by itself. An update that gave up after 10 tries only sat in the jobs list. A 503 `save_failed` from the mailbox read as "can't reach". Locked mods kept during a merge were silent. Jars only in the sharer's mods folder were left out of the share.
- After: one notice per shared pack, "Mia shared Mia's Skyblock. Get it?" with [Not now] (saved for that pack) and [Get] (normal Follow flow). A given-up update shows a warning bar at the top of the Friends panel with an outlined "Try again" button. `save_failed` says "Friends couldn't save your change just now. Hopper sends it again by itself." and keeps retrying. Locked mods kept are said once per merge. Hand-added jars are named in the share.

Mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/RpXrpF5ewpuV1Ecm2hZ5GV (picked 3B: button under the text, fits the narrow panel; 4A: one toast, Not now forever).

# Friends: someone who only shares a pack with you no longer reads "In a group with you"

**Branch:** t3/d6e07bba   **Commit:** 5c7569f   **Size:** S
**Apps:** desktop only (Hopper Server has no Friends panel)

## From -> to
- Before: a person who isn't your friend and isn't in any of your groups, but shares a pack with you directly, sat under "In your groups" with the line "In a group with you".
- After: they sit under their own heading "Sharing with you" and read "Not a friend yet". Group members still read "In a group with you" under "In your groups"; a group member who also shares directly stays under groups.

## Wording pick
- Heading **"Sharing with you"** (not "Shared with you"): it names people, and "Shared with you" sounds like a list of packs (there is a "Packs" section and "N packs for you" on the same panel).
- Subtitle **"Not a friend yet"**: says what they are and hints at the next step. "Shares a pack with you" would repeat the "· 1 pack" that already ends the row ("Shares a pack with you · 1 pack").
- Fallback name when the relay sends none: "Someone" (friends "A friend", group members "A group member").
- Order of headings: Online, Offline, In your groups, Sharing with you, Packs (search hits).

## Root cause
`mailbox::everyone()` returned `friend: Option<Friend>` only, so "not a friend" meant one thing. The panel (`FriendsPanel::everyone`, `status()` in src/gpui/friends.rs) treated every non-friend as a group member: heading "In your groups", subtitle "In a group with you". Non-friends from direct packs and non-friends from groups were told apart nowhere.

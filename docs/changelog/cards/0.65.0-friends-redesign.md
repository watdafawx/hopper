# Friends panel: groups rail (option B)

Branch `t3/friends-panel-redesign-option-a` (name kept from the first brief; the build is option B).
Screenshots in `shots/`, all at the narrowest panel width (26 rem = 390 px at 100 % zoom), with the
longest seeded names (Bartholomew_1234, "The Late Night Survival Crew", "Create: Above and Beyond,
the Long Edition", "My Very Long Modpack Name For Testing"). Test build against a local relay copy
with throwaway accounts; no real instances, accounts or relay.

## Phase 1: layout

What changed
- The Friends / Your packs tabs are gone. A rail on the left holds: Everyone (top), a line, one
  tile per group (invitations first, with an orange dot), "+" for a new group (dimmed with a reason
  when it can't be used), and Packs at the foot.
- The open tile's slot gets `row_selected` (tint + 3 px bar on the rail's edge); others `row_hover`.
  Every tile is `.clickable(cx)` (focus ring) with a tooltip naming the group.
- Tile art: the group's initials (DG, TL, WC, B, CF), like Discord without a picture; two members'
  Minecraft faces (`face_stack`) once members have real faces.
- Everyone: Online / Offline / In your groups sections; searching adds matching packs.
- Group page: name + who's in it + ⋯ on top; invitation card (Join / No thanks) when not joined;
  the "Keep my packs the same as the group" card; Packs (theirs, then yours with Change); People
  (You first, the maker, Invited chip, "+ Add" dimmed for non-makers: "Only Alex can add people").
- Packs page: Your packs (faces of who gets each + Share popover; the "every pack starts private"
  line can be closed), From friends (followed first, each pack once however it came: "From Robin and
  drunk gamblers"), "Won't change any more" inline at the bottom with Stop following.
- Header: "+ Invite" opens the invite popover (invite + join), no pinned footer.
- Empty state (nobody yet): three numbered steps, one orange "Invite a friend" (makes the code and
  shows it in the popover under the button, so the popover has no second orange button), and
  "Got a code from a friend? Paste it here" (opens only the join part under the link, input focused).
- Escape: closes a popover first, then goes back to Everyone.

Rail size: 3 rem (45 px at this rem) rail with 2.375 rem (36 px) tiles, not the mock's 56 px. At the
narrowest panel it leaves 344 px for the list: Bartholomew_1234 + "1 new" chip + chevron fit with
no clipping (p1-everyone-*.png). Not collapsible: at 45 px it doesn't crowd the list.

Found and fixed while measuring
- gpui-kit `Avatar`/`AvatarGroup` with a custom size clips the initials (its `Size::Size` branch sets
  the text box to half size and keeps the default font); its preset sizes are fixed px and don't
  follow the zoom. Tile art and pack faces use the existing rem-based `face()` + `face_stack`.
- Tiles used `muted`, which equals the panel colour in light themes: now `foreground` at 8 %.
- Two orange buttons when the popover opened from the empty state: fixed as above.
- Escape with the Share popover open left the page: fixed.

Differs from the mock
- Rail 45 px, tiles 36 px (see above).
- Faces fall back to initials, not blank heads, when members have no Minecraft face.

Tests: `cargo test -p hopper-core --lib` 1187 passed; desktop (`cargo-test-changed`) 80 passed,
incl. new `group_initials_read_like_discords` and `the_panel_never_names_the_service`.

## Phase 2: drag and drop, menus, keyboard, Undo

What changed
- Friend rows (Everyone) and member rows (group page) drag. While dragging, the rail says where
  they can go: a tile that can take them tints under the pointer; one that can't is dimmed. A card
  beside the pointer says what dropping does: "Add Alex to drunk gamblers", "Move Robin to The Late
  Night Survival Crew" (dragged off a group this PC made), or why not, with a lock: "Only Mia can
  add people", "Sam is already in The Late Night Survival Crew", "Kai isn't your friend yet", "Join
  … first", can't connect. Ctrl while dragging switches Move to Add (keeps them in the old group).
- Drop on a tile: they're invited. Toast "Alex is invited to drunk gamblers. They're in once they
  say yes." with Undo (takes the invite back). A move adds to the new group and takes them out of
  the old one after the toast's time; Undo stops both.
- Drop on "+": the new-group dialog with them ticked.
- Dragging a member of your group shows "Drop here to take Robin out of the group" under the page's
  top (it stays in view however the list is scrolled). Dropping there hides them at once and toasts
  "Robin is out of drunk gamblers." with Undo; the take-out is sent after 6 s (toast 5 s + exit).
- Menus, the keyboard way to do all of it: a friend's ⋯ has "Add to group ›" (every joined group;
  the ones that can't take them disabled with the same reason). A member's ⋯ (shown on hover or
  Tab) has "Move to ›", "Add to group ›" and "Take Robin out…". Take out from the menu asks first:
  plain dialog, a tick box "Also take Alex out of The Late Night Survival Crew" (or "of your N
  other groups") when they're in more of yours; ticking changes the button to "Take out of 2
  groups". No typing.
- Tab order: Invite, close, rail tiles (ring on each, selected one too), rows, row menus. Enter
  opens a tile or row; the ⋯ menus work with arrows.
- Drag/menu edits use a new quiet `Store::edit_members`: the panel's toast says it, so the store's
  own "Invited 1 friend…" notice no longer stacks on top (seen in testing, fixed).

Found and fixed while checking
- The drag ghost sat left of the pointer (GPUI draws it at the grab offset), clipping the hint:
  it's now shifted by the grab point, card 14 px right / 10 px below the pointer.
- Dark: ghost name was black on the dark card (the drag is drawn outside the tree, inherits no
  colour): now `popover_foreground`.
- Dark: zone red (220,38,38) read 3.78:1: lifted to L ≥ 0.68, now 6.15:1.
- Tiles only took drops after a frame with the drag drawn (a quick flick missed): drops now always
  register and re-check.
- Zone also showed a hint card running off the window's right edge: removed (the zone says it).

Test tooling (separate commit): MCP `drag` gained `hold` / `resume` to screenshot mid-drag, and
its mouse events now carry held `modifiers` (Ctrl+drop), as `click` already did.

Differs from the mock
- The hint is a card beside the pointer, not a tooltip on the tile (tooltips don't show while
  dragging in GPUI).
- Member rows have a ⋯ menu instead of the ✕: the take-out moved into it with Move / Add.

Tests: `cargo test -p hopper-core --lib` 1187 passed; desktop (`cargo-test-changed`) 80 passed.
hopperd's `built_page_has_no_inline_script_or_style` fails here because this worktree has no built
web UI (`ui/dist`); it's pulled in by the hopper-core MCP change, not related.

## Phase 3: sync status chip, status card, "changed by" lines

What changed (src/gpui/friends.rs):
- Header chip beside "Friends" shows the followed pack that needs the player most:
  "Couldn't update" (red), "Needs your answer" (amber), "After the game" (grey),
  "Syncing" (orange), "In sync" (green). Tooltip names the pack and the reason (and
  "N more need a look"). Click opens Packs. No chip when Friends is off, can't connect,
  or nothing is followed. Focus ring via `clickable`.
- Packs page: a tinted card on top when a followed pack failed or waits for an answer:
  pack name, the reason (failed: "... Trying again soon."), and Update (held mods) or Open.
- Rail badges: `TileMark::Alert` (red failed / amber needs answer) on the group tile the
  pack syncs through and on the Packs tile; tooltips say "a pack needs a look".
- "Changed by" line under group packs (both the friend's pack row and the followed row):
  "Mia turned off 2 mods · 3 h ago", from the group copy's newest mark not made here.
- Held packs no longer say "up to date" next to the "needs your answer" note.

How states were made: settings seeded by scratch `p3set.py` (held mods + a group copy with
Mia's marks); the failed state is real: fake Alex shared a broken new code through the local
relay, Hopper tried, failed, and is retrying.

mod key, not name), so it counts them: "Mia turned off 2 mods". The card lists only packs that
need the player; waiting-for-the-game stays in the chip.

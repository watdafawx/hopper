# Friends and History: no fake "Offline", clean face rows, "put back" for undo

**Branch:** t3/history-friends-status-polish   **Commit:** 0732c5f   **Size:** M
**Apps:** both (Friends status is desktop only: Hopper Server has no friends page)

## From -> to
- Before: group members who aren't your friends always read "Offline"; History rows by 2-3 people showed small faces squeezed and overlapping in one face's space; an Undo entry read "You switched options.txt".
- After: non-friends show no status, a plain avatar and a tooltip "Not your friend: status hidden"; 2-3 faces sit side by side at full size with nested rows lined up under the text; Undo entries read "You put back options.txt".

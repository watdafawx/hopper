# Groups: members who aren't friends get each other's changes (tests only)

**Branch:** t3/group-sync-nonfriend-members   **Commit:** 85475c1   **Size:** S
**Apps:** none changed. Tests only (desktop group sync lives in `crates/hopper-core`).

## From -> to
- Before: nothing checked that a group member you aren't friends with gets your changes, or that you get theirs.
- After: no app change. Three new tests cover A-B and B-C friends, A and C not, in a group B made: the relay (Python, real server on loopback), the desktop's mailbox client against a local relay, and the group sync simulation with three PCs and a late fourth member who only knows B. Sync is addressed by group membership, not by friendship.

## Known limits
- Only the group's owner can invite people, and only from the owner's friends.
- A member who isn't your friend shows as "Offline".

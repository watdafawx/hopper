## Requirements

This resource pack is made possible with an OptiFine feature called custom entity models (CEM) to add new entity models and animations. 

**Entity Model Features and Entity Texture Features (EMF & ETF) is recommended as an alternative to OptiFine.**
  
[<img src="https://imgur.com/QN7fhnN.png" alt="EMF" width="50"/>](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features) 
[<img src="https://imgur.com/UAeSDHd.png" alt="ETF" width="50"/>](https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures)

## Extension Packs

Fresh Animations Extensions are extra resource packs with additional features for Fresh Animations.

-   Load these packs above Fresh Animations to work correctly.
-   These packs are mostly suited for the vanilla look and aren't friendly to other resource packs.
-   Most are made for and require Fresh Animations.

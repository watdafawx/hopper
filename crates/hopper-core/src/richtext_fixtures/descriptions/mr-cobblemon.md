<p><center><span><font size="5">Cobblemon is an open-source Pok&eacute;mon mod for Fabric and NeoForge.</font></span></center></p>
<p><span>The goal of&nbsp;Cobblemon&nbsp;is to be the Pok&eacute;mon mod for everyone, and to integrate much better into modpacks and Minecraft itself than other Pok&eacute;mon mods. Our source code is open to encourage not only community contributions, but also forking.</span></p>

<p><center><span><font size="4"><strong><em>Features (so far!):</em></strong></font></span></center></p>
<ul>
<li><span>Capture Pok&eacute;mon to expand your team, battle wild Pok&eacute;mon to gain experience, level up to unlock new moves, and evolve your Pok&eacute;mon. There are over 800 Pokémon to catch!</span></li>
<li><span>Battles using the <a title="https://pokemonshowdown.com" href="https://pokemonshowdown.com"><span><em>Pok&eacute;mon Showdown!</em></span></a> engine for accurate competitive battle mechanics.</span></li>
<li><span>Some<em> Legends Arceus</em>-style&nbsp;mechanics, such as move relearning, physically running from battle, and evolving your Pok&eacute;mon when <em>you</em> decide.</span></li>
<li><span>Toggle free-movement during battles. You can build, mine, or fight mobs while in Pok&eacute;mon battles!</span></li>
<li><span>Record the Pokémon you see and catch with a Pokédex. They come with a scanning feature to analyze wild Pokémon!</span></li>
<li><span>Explore the Minecraft world in style by riding your Pokémon!</span></li>
<li><span>Engage in tough battles with Alpha Pokémon that can be caught or defeated for rewards!</span></li>
<li><span>You can trade and battle with your friends in multiplayer.</span></li>
<li><span>Let your Pokémon roam free wherever you want using pastures!</span></li>
<li><span>Berries which can be found in the wild, grown on farmland, mulched for bonuses, and cross-bred to mutate new berries.</span></li>
<li><span>Medicine items can be made by putting crops and berries into a brewing stand. Brewed potions heal more!</span></li>
<li><span>Ancient structures in the world to discover. Some of these structures have gilded chests, which may contain valuable loot or other surprises!</span></li>
<li><span>Fossils can be found by excavating suspicious gravel or sand at certain locations, which can then be revived using a resurrection machine.</span></li>
<li><span>All Pokémon from generations 1 to 9 can be spawned and battled with, but if they don't have a model yet then they will display as a substitute doll.</span></li>

A complete redesign of Minecraft's dungeons like never before. YUNG's Better Dungeons redesigns vanilla dungeons while also adding three new dungeons: <b>Catacombs, Fortresses of the Undead, and Spider Caves!</b>
</p>
<p>
<b>1.19.2 introduces the Nether Update! </b>Now you can find small Nether dungeons scattered throughout the Nether.<b> Note that this is an extra opt-in feature</b>, meaning you will need to manually enable it in the mod's config.
